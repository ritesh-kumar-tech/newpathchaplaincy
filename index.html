About Us

A Global Chaplaincy Network Built for Service

We connect compassionate individuals with the training, community, and professional support needed to serve people during life's most important moments.
Global ministry with practical care

Training, connecting, credentialing, and supporting chaplains for moments that matter.

Newpath brings spiritual care, emotional support, and practical hope into churches, communities, institutions, and crisis response environments.

01

Mission

To train, connect, credential, and support chaplains who bring spiritual care, emotional support, and practical hope to people in need.

02

Vision

To build a global community of qualified chaplains serving with compassion, integrity, cultural wisdom, and professional readiness.

03

Core Values

Faith, confidentiality, excellence, servant leadership, emergency readiness, accountability, and care for all people.

Ministerial Licensing

Professional Chaplain Licensing with Clear Standards

Our structured licensing process supports professional accountability, ministry credibility, and responsible service.

  1. 01 ApplySubmit your application and ministry information.
  2. 02 ReviewThe Newpath review team evaluates eligibility and next steps.
  3. 03 VerifyDocuments, ministry background, and required information are verified.
  4. 04 LicenseApproved applicants receive follow-up guidance and licensing communication.

What to Prepare

  • Ministry or church affiliation summary
  • Training and certification records
  • Experience and service background
  • Current contact and follow-up details
